## 1. Received differential voltage with correct (100 Ω) and wrong (50 Ω) termination. This tests the post's 4/9 × VOD ≈ 109.8 mV minimum and its 1/2 × VOD settled value under the ideal-current-source, electrically-long-line model.

- **준비:** One SN65LVDS31 driver channel feeding one SN65LVDS32 receiver channel, either on the TI EVM or on the author's own board. The datasheet lot or date code goes in the record. Use a known twisted-pair or ribbon cable (record the type, length in metres and the stated Zdiff), or a PCB pair of known length. Use an oscilloscope with a differential probe or two matched single-ended probes at ≥1 GHz bandwidth, and record the scope and probe models. Drive with a 1–10 MHz square wave from a function generator or an MCU/FPGA pin into the driver input. Record VCC.
- **절차:** 1) Probe directly across the receiver A–B pins with a 100 Ω 0603 resistor at the receiver. Capture a single-shot rising edge and a falling edge, then measure the settled |VID| and the driver-side VOD. 2) Replace the resistor with 50 Ω (or two 100 Ω in parallel) and repeat. Capture long enough to see the step sequence on the first edge (2/3, 4/9, 14/27 ... VOD). Measure the first step, the lowest step and the settled value. 3) Measure the driver-side 10–90% edge time and the cable's one-way delay. Get the delay from the time between the driver edge and the receiver edge, or calculate it from the cable's velocity factor. 4) Repeat step 2 with a cable short enough that the round trip is below the edge time. This shows the stepped waveform turning into a smooth one, which is the electrically-long-line condition the reviewer asked for.
- **기록할 것:** Each of these numbers in mV, plus screenshots: VOD at the driver; |VID| settled at the receiver with 100 Ω; with 50 Ω, the first step, the minimum and the settled value. Also record the edge time in ns, the cable one-way delay in ns, the cable length and type, VCC and the probe/scope models. Note whether the measured steps track 2/3 → 4/9 → 1/2 of the measured VOD (not of the datasheet 247 mV) and how far they differ. Note the result of the short-cable comparison.
- **글에 넣을 위치:** Section '전류 모드 드라이버와 100 Ω 종단', directly after the 50 Ω calculation paragraph (L50). Add a short measurement paragraph or table plus one capture image. Change the summary bullet L138 so it states the model assumption and points to the measured values.
- **오르는 항목:** C (moves from calculation-only to 'actual measurement, environment difference recorded', toward 12–15). It also answers the reviewer's C issue at L50/L138 about stating the electrically-long-line condition. ## 3. Reflection from a differential-pair spacing change (S = 2H to S = H microstrip section). Measure both the reflected magnitude at the discontinuity and the amplitude disturbance that actually arrives at the receiver, to settle the reviewer's question about how the computed 16 mV enters the receiver budget.

- **준비:** A test PCB (or coupon) with a 100 Ω edge-coupled microstrip pair at S = 2H and one section of known length at S = H, same width. Record the stackup H, W, T, εr from the fab and the section length. Measure with a TDR (scope TDR module or VNA in time domain) with differential stimulus. If none is available, use the SN65LVDS31 driving the coupon terminated with 100 Ω and a fast scope at both ends. Record the instrument, rise time and calibration.
- **절차:** 1) Use differential TDR to measure Zdiff of the 2H section and of the H section, and the reflected step amplitude at the discontinuity as ρ. 2) With the SN65LVDS31 driving the coupon and the 100 Ω receiver end, capture the waveform at the receiver pins. Measure the disturbance amplitude (ISI bump/notch) arriving after the re-reflection, and when it arrives relative to the edge. 3) As a control, capture the same on a uniform coupon without the narrow section.
- **기록할 것:** Zdiff of each section in Ω (measured vs. calculated 100 / 87.8 Ω); measured ρ; reflected amplitude in mV at the driver end; disturbance amplitude in mV and delay in ns at the receiver, compared with the uniform control. Also record stackup values, section length and TDR rise time (it limits what a short section shows).
- **글에 넣을 위치:** Section '차동 임피던스 — 배선 형상이 예산을 얼마나 쓰나', directly after L61. Replace or qualify '예산의 약 11%' with the measured arrival amplitude, and update summary bullet L139 the same way.
- **오르는 항목:** C (measurement plus a trade-off: a calculated reflection vs. what reaches the receiver). A: an original finding on whether the geometry rule matters at 100 Mbps edge rates. It resolves the A/B issue at L61/L139 with data instead of rewording.
